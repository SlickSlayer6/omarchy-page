How inner pages inherit this chrome

A note that goes with the homepage redesign. Not a second product. The homepage is the piece; this is how the rest of omarchy.org would wear it.

Inner pages today all open with the full ASCII mural, then a one-line header, then the content. That mark is the brand, and it should stay the brand — just not as a ten-line billboard above every manual chapter, news post, and team roster. It eats the fold on a phone, and it keeps the actual page from starting.

What carries over

  • The compact bar: wordmark, a handful of text links, optional theme dots. This page is wearing it.
  • The CSS variables in root.css. Catppuccin is the default. A later theme pass is data-theme on <html>, not a restyle of every article.
  • JetBrains Mono, the radius, the buttons, the notification strip when there’s news.

What does not get redesigned here

  • The manual’s sidebar, search, and pagination.
  • The news cards.
  • The extra-themes screenshot grid.
  • The workstations lightbox.
  • Teams, patrons, meetups, security, AIR.

Those layouts are fine. They swap the mural for this bar, keep their existing h1 (The Extra Themes, The teams guiding Omarchy, …), and otherwise stay put. The homepage was the thing that had outgrown itself. The inside of the site mostly needs a hat that fits.

Theme dots on inner pages

Optional. The homepage should keep them because the product is the theme. Documentation can freeze on Tokyo Night so code samples and screenshots don’t drift under the reader. The tokens still mean a global pass is cheap if you want it later. Click the dots on this page to see the bar and type recolor without a desktop screenshot.

Drop-in

This is static HTML and CSS, the same shape as omarchy-site today. The homepage is index.html. No framework. The wordmark is the official ASCII, shrunk into the bar via the existing SVG in /brand.
